[h1][B42] Bus Stop Fast Travel[/h1]

Adds admin-placed bus stops to any server. Players walk up, pick a destination, pay the fare, and get teleported with a fade transition. Fully server-authoritative.

[b]Your bus network survives everything.[/b] Stops are saved to their own server file, completely separate from the save itself — starting a brand new world, wiping a region, or updating the mod never wipes your routes. Set them up once, keep them forever.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How It Works[/h2]

[b]Players:[/b] walk up to a stop, right-click and choose [b]Use bus stop[/b], pick a destination in the travel panel, then confirm to travel. Screen fades out, you're teleported, fades back in. Destinations with [b]Return Trip[/b] get a free one-click return on your next visit; [b]Random Trip[/b] sends you somewhere random if enabled. Holding a [b]Bus Ticket[/b] item pays for the trip for free (consumed on use) at any stop that accepts it.

[b]Admins:[/b] right-click any tile to [b]Build bus stop here[/b], right-click a stop to [b]Remove[/b] it, or right-click anywhere to open [b]Manage bus stops[/b] for the full admin panel — edit any field, wipe every stop at once with [b]Clear All[/b], or mark a stop [b]Arrival Only[/b] to make it a one-way destination.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Persistent across saves[/b] — stops live in their own server file, not the save; new worlds, soft-wipes, and mod updates never lose your routes. If a stop's physical sign isn't there yet (new world, wiped region, or a preset stop nobody has visited), stand within a few tiles and [b]right-click that exact tile[/b] once — the sign appears automatically and the stop works from then on
[*][b]Ready out of the box[/b] — a fresh server with no stops yet starts pre-seeded with 14 stops covering every major town (Muldraugh, Rosewood, Riverside, West Point, Louisville, and more) with dynamic pricing; their signs aren't placed until someone right-clicks the exact spot for the first time (see above), so don't worry if they're not visible immediately after installing. Edit, disable, or delete any of them from the admin panel, or wipe them with [b]Clear All[/b] if you'd rather build your own network from scratch
[*][b]Travel panel[/b] — searchable, scrollable destination list; select then confirm, no accidental trips
[*][b]One-way stops[/b] — mark a stop [b]Arrival Only[/b] so it can be reached but never departed from; shown in red on the map so it's easy to spot
[*][b]Random Trip[/b] — configurable price, optional toggle
[*][b]Fade transition[/b] — full-screen fade with bus sound effects, adjustable volume
[*][b]Arrival protection[/b] — brief zombie immunity with a countdown HUD after teleporting
[*][b]Pricing[/b] — Free, Fixed, or Dynamic (distance-based) fares, with a per-stop multiplier
[*][b]Economy[/b] — pay with an inventory item, a bank mod (simple 3-function API), or both; includes a Survivor Shop bridge example
[*][b]Bus Ticket item[/b] — findable in schools, offices, houses, and on zombies; pays for one trip free; each stop can accept or reject it; fully configurable via sandbox
[*][b]Admin panel[/b] — edit any stop's fields inline, teleport to it, delete it, or wipe every stop at once with [b]Clear All[/b] (confirmation required)
[*][b]Map markers[/b] — every active stop shown as an icon on the map, color-coded by status
[/list]
